# QA Interview Prep

## Summary

Common testing interview questions with model answer outlines and the tricky follow-ups interviewers use: "How would you test a lift / a login page / an API?", severity vs priority, what to automate first, how to handle a bug a developer rejects, how to test with no requirements. Answers link to the sections that back them up.

## Why it fits

Many visitors learning without a certificate want a job or a promotion. The content already exists across tracks; this frames it the way interviews ask. Soft Skills chapter 4 covers interview preparation in general.

## Scope

- `src/data/learning/interview.js`: question, what the interviewer looks for, answer outline, follow-ups, links
- Interview prep page with filters by level (junior, mid, senior) and area
- Practice mode: question first, answer hidden until you've thought it through
