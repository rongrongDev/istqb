# Capstone Projects (Multi-Skill Case Work)

## Summary

Longer case-based projects built around a realistic brief — launching a payment feature, migrating a monolith, releasing a mobile banking app — with staged tasks across risk analysis, test design, performance and security planning, and reporting. Each stage has a model answer to compare with, tying many sections together the way real work does.

## Why it fits

Exercises cover one technique each; paths are reading lists. Capstones combine them and reuse the risk register, test plan and performance plan from the Templates library.

## Scope

- `src/data/learning/capstones.js`: brief, stages with tasks, model answers, templates to use, links
- Capstone page with stage-by-stage progress (stored locally)
- Start with 2–3 projects at different levels

## Risks & unknowns

- Large authoring effort; model answers must show reasoning and alternatives
