# Exam Technique Playbook

## Summary

A guide to answering ISTQB-style questions: reading the last line first, spotting K-level cues, eliminating options, handling "select TWO", budgeting time by points, when to flag and move on, and how scenario questions hide the key fact. Each tactic is shown on a real sample question.

## Why it fits

Learners currently get content and questions but no method for the exam itself. The playbook links to existing official questions as worked demonstrations; no new questions needed.

## Scope

- Playbook page (or a section on each track's Insights step) with 10–12 tactics
- Each tactic: rule, why it works, one annotated official question
- Link from the QuizPage check-in ("Read the playbook first")

## Risks & unknowns

- Tactics must not encourage guessing over understanding
