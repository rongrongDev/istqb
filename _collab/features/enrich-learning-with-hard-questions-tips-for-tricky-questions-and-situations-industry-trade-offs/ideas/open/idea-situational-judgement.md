# Tricky Situations: What Would You Do?

## Summary

Branching workplace scenarios a tester actually faces: a critical bug found the day before release, a developer who says "works on my machine", a flaky pipeline blocking everyone, a PO who wants to skip regression, no test environment, a manager asking for 100% automation. You choose a response, see its consequences, and read an expert's reasoning and the syllabus ideas behind it.

## Why it fits

Soft Skills, CT-AcT collaboration and CTFL test management cover the theory; nothing lets learners practise judgement. Scenarios link back to those sections.

## Scope

- `src/data/learning/situations.js`: scenario, 3–4 responses with consequences and a rating, expert commentary, links
- Situations page; one scenario per view with reveal after choosing
- Start with 8–10 scenarios across test management, communication, automation and release

## Risks & unknowns

- There's rarely one right answer — ratings must explain trade-offs, not just mark right/wrong
