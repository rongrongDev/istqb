# Explain It Back (Self-Explanation Prompts)

## Summary

After a section, prompt the learner to explain the idea in their own words ("Explain to a developer why boundary values matter"), then show a model answer and a checklist of key points to compare against. Self-explanation is one of the most effective ways to make understanding stick.

## Why it fits

Learn sections end with "Mark studied" and nothing checks understanding without an exam question. Answers stay in the browser; no backend or grading needed.

## Scope

- `prompt`, `keyPoints[]` and `model` per selected section
- Text box, then reveal the model answer and tick the key points covered
- Saved answers viewable later (fits the personal-notes idea in exam-prep-tools)

## Risks & unknowns

- Writing prompts for hundreds of sections is heavy — start with core K2/K3 sections
