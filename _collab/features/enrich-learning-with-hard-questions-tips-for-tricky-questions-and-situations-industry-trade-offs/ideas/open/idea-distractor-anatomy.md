# Distractor Anatomy: Why Wrong Answers Look Right

## Summary

Tag every wrong option in the official sample questions with the kind of trap it is — true but irrelevant, reversed definition, too absolute ("always", "never"), wrong test level, swapped term, wrong K-level answer. Review then shows the trap type, and a per-learner tally reveals which traps catch you most.

## Why it fits

The official explanations already say why each option is wrong ("WRONG (b): …"); the tags make the pattern explicit and countable. Works across all 13 tracks' existing quizzes.

## Scope

- A small trap taxonomy (6–8 types) with a short guide page
- `trap` tag per wrong option in quiz data (authored, starting with CTFL)
- Trap chip in QuizPage review; "traps that caught you" summary after practice and mocks

## Risks & unknowns

- Tagging ~1,000 wrong options across tracks is a large authoring job
- Some options fit two trap types
