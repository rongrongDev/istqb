# Advanced Level Test Management (CTAL-TM v3.0)

## Summary

Add the Advanced Test Management syllabus (v3.0, the only English version since 30 May 2025): test management in context, risk management, product quality and defect management, team and stakeholder management. It suits the test-lead and manager part of the audience.

## Why it fits

It opens a management path on the hub alongside the technical ones. The soft-skills track (CT-SS) already covers communication and stakeholder topics that TM builds on, and CTFL ch. 5 (managing test activities) is its foundation. Follows the established track recipe from the done tasks (CTFL v4, CT-QDO, CT-MAT, CT-SS): `src/data/<id>/` (chapters + learn content + official sample-exam quiz checked against the answer key + glossary), five step pages (Learn / Summaries / Exam Insights / Glossary / Quiz with a timed mock), a `makeProgress` storage key, and one `tracks.js` entry, which makes it appear on the hub, the track page and the breadcrumbs automatically.

## Scope

- All v3.0 chapters, learn content, objectives
- Sample exam quiz with scenario questions
- Glossary; links to CTFL ch. 5 and CT-SS where they overlap
- New `tracks.js` entry, progress key, five step pages, hub card
