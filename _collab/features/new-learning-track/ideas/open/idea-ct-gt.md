# Gambling Industry Tester (CT-GT)

## Summary

Add ISTQB's Gambling Industry Tester specialist syllabus: testing regulated gambling systems (lottery, casino, sports betting), including compliance, randomness/RNG, payouts and jurisdiction-specific rules.

## Why it fits

Like the approved Finance Testing track, it's a regulated-domain specialist cert, and the two could share a “domain” grouping on the hub. Follows the established track recipe from the done tasks (CTFL v4, CT-QDO, CT-MAT, CT-SS): `src/data/<id>/` (chapters + learn content + official sample-exam quiz checked against the answer key + glossary), five step pages (Learn / Summaries / Exam Insights / Glossary / Quiz with a timed mock), a `makeProgress` storage key, and one `tracks.js` entry, which makes it appear on the hub, the track page and the breadcrumbs automatically.

## Scope

- New `tracks.js` entry, progress key, five step pages, hub card
