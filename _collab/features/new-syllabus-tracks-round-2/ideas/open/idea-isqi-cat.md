# Certified Agile Tester (iSQI CAT)

## Summary

A track for iSQI's Certified Agile Tester: agile testing in practice, team roles, iteration testing and exploratory work.

## Why it fits

Adds an agile-practice option next to the open CTFL-AT and CTAL-ATT ideas, for teams that use the iSQI scheme.

## Scope

- Standard track recipe

## Risks & unknowns

- CAT includes a practical exam that the site cannot simulate
- Overlap with CTFL-AT
