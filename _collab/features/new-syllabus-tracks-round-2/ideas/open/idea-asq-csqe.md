# Certified Software Quality Engineer (ASQ CSQE)

## Summary

A track for ASQ's Certified Software Quality Engineer body of knowledge: quality management, SDLC processes, metrics, verification and validation, configuration management. It's for quality engineers whose role is wider than testing.

## Why it fits

Answers the "quality engineer" audience directly; complements ISTQB's test-centric tracks.

## Scope

- Track recipe adapted to a body of knowledge instead of a syllabus with LOs
- Metrics and formulas as learn blocks

## Risks & unknowns

- ASQ publishes a body of knowledge, not a syllabus with K-levels; official sample questions are limited
