# Agile Test Leadership at Scale (CT-ATLaS v2.0)

## Summary

A full study track for ISTQB CT-ATLaS v2.0 (2023): test leadership and quality coaching in agile organisations at scale. It suits test leads and coaches who have outgrown the Foundation and specialist tracks.

## Why it fits

The CT-FT syllabus cites [ISTQB_CT-ATLaS_SYL] v2.0, so it is current. It covers leadership ground none of the 13 tracks (or the open CTFL-AT/CTAL-ATT ideas) cover. It follows the same recipe as tasks 001–008 on the Exam Room shared pages.

## Scope

- `src/data/ctatlas/` chapters, learn content, quiz from the official sample exam, glossary
- Five thin pages and a `tracks.js` entry
- Sources from `trainings/` like the last batch

## Risks & unknowns

- Leadership-style content is less "technical", so K-level questions are scenario-heavy
- Need the syllabus and sample exam PDFs uploaded (istqb.org is blocked here)
