# Requirements Engineering Foundation (IREB CPRE-FL)

## Summary

A track for IREB's Certified Professional for Requirements Engineering, Foundation Level. Testers, BAs and product owners learn to elicit, document and validate requirements — the test basis every ISTQB syllabus assumes.

## Why it fits

Requirement quality drives CT-AcT chapter 1 and CTFL static testing. It's a non-ISTQB scheme but widely held by testers, and it is the first track outside ISTQB/ASTQB.

## Scope

- Same track recipe as tasks 001–008
- Badge/org shown as IREB, not ISTQB
- Cross-links to CT-AcT and CTFL

## Risks & unknowns

- Different exam format and sample-exam availability; licensing of IREB material needs checking
