# Personal Learning Record

## Summary

A page summarising what someone has studied across all tracks and paths (sections read, paths completed, exercises done) that they can screenshot or share as a learning log for a manager or performance review — with no certificate involved.

## Why it fits

Progress already lives per track in localStorage; this aggregates it and adds paths/exercises.

## Scope

- Aggregate view across tracks and paths with dates
- Shareable summary card (image or link encoding counts only)

## Risks & unknowns

- Not verifiable: it's self-reported study activity, and must say so
