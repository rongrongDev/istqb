# Concept of the Day

## Summary

One short concept card each day (a term, a technique or a heuristic) with a one-minute explanation and a link to read more, shown on the dashboard. It builds a light learning habit for busy engineers.

## Why it fits

Glossaries and Learn sections provide an endless pool; the dashboard is the natural place.

## Scope

- Deterministic daily pick (date-seeded) across tracks or the learner's chosen paths
- "Seen" history in localStorage
- Optional filter by level or topic

## Risks & unknowns

- Random picks may be too advanced for beginners without the level tags
