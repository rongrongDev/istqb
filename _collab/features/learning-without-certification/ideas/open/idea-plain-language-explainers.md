# Plain-Language Explainers and Jargon Tooltips

## Summary

A short "In plain words" box on dense sections, and dotted-underline tooltips on glossary terms inside Learn text that show the definition on hover or tap. Readers new to testing aren't stopped by jargon.

## Why it fits

Glossaries exist for every track, and ⌘K lookup already resolves terms; tooltips bring that inline.

## Scope

- Auto-link glossary terms in Learn paragraphs (first occurrence per section)
- Optional `plain` field per section with a 2–3 sentence explanation
- Tooltip works by tap on phones

## Risks & unknowns

- Auto-linking can over-match common words ("risk", "test")
