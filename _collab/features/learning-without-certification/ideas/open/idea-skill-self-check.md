# Where Do I Start? Skill Self-Check

## Summary

A short diagnostic (10–15 questions written for this purpose, not exam questions) that places a visitor at beginner, practitioner or expert per area — test design, automation, performance, security, usability — and recommends paths. Newcomers get a starting point in two minutes.

## Why it fits

Pairs with role paths and level tags; uses the site's own content as recommendations. Unlike the open certification picker, it measures knowledge rather than goals.

## Scope

- Self-check questions per area with clear labels that they are diagnostic only
- Result: radar of areas and recommended paths
- Result stored locally, retakable

## Risks & unknowns

- Writing a fair diagnostic is hard; it must stay clearly separate from official sample questions
