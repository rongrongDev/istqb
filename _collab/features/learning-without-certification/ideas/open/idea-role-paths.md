# Learning Paths by Role

## Summary

Starting points for developers, product owners, business analysts, engineering managers, new QA engineers and SDETs, each picking the sections that matter for that role. Non-testers find a way in without wading through exam material.

## Why it fits

The open "Which certification next?" picker recommends certificates; this recommends content for people who don't want one.

## Scope

- Role picker on the dashboard
- Role → list of topic paths and key sections
- Plain intro per role explaining why testing knowledge helps them

## Risks & unknowns

- Overlap with topic paths; roles may just be bundles of topic paths
