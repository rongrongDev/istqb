# Official Errata Applied per Track

## Summary

Record official ISTQB/ASTQB errata and answer-key corrections per track, show which have been applied, and note where the site deliberately differs (e.g. a sample answer we believe is wrong). Learners trust that known errors are handled.

## Why it fits

This session couldn't check errata online; sample answer keys have had corrections (CT-AcT answers are v1.3). Errata today would be silently applied with no trace.

## Scope

- `errata` field per track: source, date, items, applied yes/no
- Shown in the content notes panel and quiz review
- Checklist step in the syllabus update routine

## Risks & unknowns

- Finding errata requires access to istqb.org/astqb.org
