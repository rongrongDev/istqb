# Objectives Without Sample Questions

## Summary

On each track page and in Insights, list the learning objectives that no official sample question covers (e.g. several CT-UT 5.x objectives). Learners know where the sample exams give no practice, so they study those from Learn instead of assuming they are safe.

## Why it fits

Questions carry `lo` and chapters carry objectives, so the gap list can be computed. Sample exams cover only part of each syllabus.

## Scope

- Compute objectives with zero questions per track
- Show them in Insights with a "not in sample exams" chip
- Link each to its Learn section

## Risks & unknowns

- Topic-mapped LOs (CT-SEC, CT-UT keyword questions) blur the picture
