# Link Terms to the Official ISTQB Glossary

## Summary

Each glossary entry that also exists in the official ISTQB glossary gets a link to its page on glossary.istqb.org. Learners can check the authoritative wording, which is what the exam uses.

## Why it fits

The site's glossary definitions are paraphrased from syllabi; several tracks say exams test exact definitions. A link closes that gap without copying the official text.

## Scope

- Map term → official glossary URL/slug (manual or a one-off script)
- "Official definition ↗" link on GlossaryPage and ⌘K results
- Mark terms that are syllabus-only (domain terms)

## Risks & unknowns

- URL scheme of glossary.istqb.org may change; istqb.org is unreachable from this environment
