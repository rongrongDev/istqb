# Section-by-Section Reader Mode

## Summary

An alternative Learn view that shows one section at a time, with Previous / Next buttons, a chapter progress bar (“Section 3 of 9”) and keyboard arrows. Finishing a section moves you on, instead of scrolling one very long page of every section.

## Why it fits

The Learn page renders all of a chapter's sections in one scroll (CTFL ch. 5 learn content is 256 lines; CT-AI v1 has 11 chapters), with only a “‹ All chapters” button at the top. Sections already have ids, titles, K-levels and a studied toggle, so the unit of reading exists.

## Scope

- Toggle between “Full chapter” and “One section at a time”, remembered per browser
- Prev/next across chapter boundaries (end of ch. 2 → start of ch. 3)
- Sticky mini table of contents (drawer on mobile) with studied ticks

## Risks & unknowns

- Scope doubles unless done once in a shared Learn template
