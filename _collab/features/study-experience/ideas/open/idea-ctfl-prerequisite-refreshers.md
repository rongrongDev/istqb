# CTFL Prerequisite Refreshers in Specialist Tracks

## Summary

In the specialist tracks (CT-AI, CT-QDO, CT-MAT), flag the places where the syllabus assumes Foundation knowledge (e.g. “regression testing”, “test levels”, “risk-based testing”) and link each to a short CTFL refresher pop-out or the matching CTFL Learn section.

## Why it fits

Four of the five tracks list “CTFL certificate” as the prerequisite in `tracks.js`, and the CTFL track's Learn content and glossary already live on the same site. Specialist chapters reuse Foundation vocabulary without re-explaining it.

## Scope

- Per-track mapping file: specialist section/keyword → CTFL section id(s)
- “Foundation refresher” chip in the specialist Learn section, opening a pop-out with the CTFL definition and a link
- Track page note: “Rusty on Foundation? Start with these 6 CTFL sections”

## Risks & unknowns

- The mapping is manual curation per track
- Overlaps with the earlier glossary-tooltip idea at the term level; this one is about cross-track sections
