# Interactive K3 Technique Workbench

## Summary

Hands-on exercises for the “apply” objectives: given a spec, partition inputs (equivalence partitioning), pick boundary values (BVA 2-value / 3-value), fill a decision table, walk a state-transition diagram, compute coverage. Answers are checked instantly with hints. K3 questions are where most CTFL candidates lose points.

## Why it fits

The CTFL guide itself says “The 8 K3 objectives (EP, BVA, decision tables, state transitions, ATDD, estimation, prioritization, defect reports) each yield an apply-style question — practice them on scenarios”, but the site offers no way to practise apart from the fixed sample questions. Learn sections for 4.2.x already explain the techniques.

## Scope

- Exercise generators for EP, BVA (2- and 3-value), decision tables, state transitions, statement/branch coverage
- Embedded at the end of the relevant Learn sections, plus a standalone workbench page per track
- Step-by-step worked solution after an attempt

## Risks & unknowns

- Generated exercises must be correct by construction; needs careful logic and tests
- CTFL-specific at first; other tracks have fewer K3 objectives
