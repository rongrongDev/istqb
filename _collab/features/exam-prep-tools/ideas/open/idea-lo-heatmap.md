# Learning-Objective Heatmap

## Summary

A per-track view showing how you score on each learning objective (FT-5.3, AcT-2.2.2…) and K-level, across practice answers and mock exams. Learners see exactly which objectives they keep missing, at a finer grain than chapters.

## Why it fits

Every quiz question already carries `lo`, `level` and `chapter`. The root backlog's attempt history and weak-spot drill work per chapter; this goes one level down, using data that's already there.

## Scope

- Record per-question outcomes (practice and exam) per track
- Grid of objectives grouped by chapter, coloured by % correct, with K-level chips
- Click an objective → its Learn section and a practice set of just its questions

## Risks & unknowns

- Some LOs have only 1–2 questions, so percentages are noisy
- CT-SEC and CT-UT keyword questions have LOs mapped by topic, not officially
