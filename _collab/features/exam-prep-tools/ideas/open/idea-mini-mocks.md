# 10-Question Mini Mocks

## Summary

A short timed round of 10 random questions (about 15 minutes) drawn across chapters in proportion to the real exam. It fits a lunch break when a 60–120 minute mock is too long.

## Why it fits

QuizPage offers untimed practice or a full timed exam, nothing in between. Mini mocks reuse the same question pools, timer and review screens.

## Scope

- "Mini mock" card on the quiz check-in
- Chapter-weighted random draw, timer scaled to the official pace
- Result with per-chapter dots; not counted as a best mock

## Risks & unknowns

- Random draws repeat questions quickly on single-set tracks
