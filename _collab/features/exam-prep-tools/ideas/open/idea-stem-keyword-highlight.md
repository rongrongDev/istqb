# Highlight Tricky Words in Question Stems

## Summary

Optionally highlight words that flip a question's meaning — NOT, LEAST, BEST, MOST, FIRST, "Select TWO" — in question stems during practice. Learners build the habit of spotting them before the real exam, where nothing is highlighted.

## Why it fits

Sample exams use these words constantly ("Which is the LEAST meaningful argument…", CT-AcT Q23), and Insights already flags TRAP items. This brings that coaching into the quiz.

## Scope

- Toggle in Practice mode (off in exam simulation)
- Word list per language-agnostic regex
- Short tip on first use explaining why

## Risks & unknowns

- Could become a crutch if left on in mocks
