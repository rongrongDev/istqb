# Brainstorm: exam-prep-tools

Exam prep tools — practice, revision and study features on the Exam Room pages.

To continue: open any idea file(s) in ideas/open/, fill in the `> YOUR ANSWER:`
line(s), save, then run `/tome:brainstorm exam-prep-tools` again.

To drop an idea, change its `## Status` line to REJECTED.
To park an idea for later, change its `## Status` line to KIV.

Ideas already planned elsewhere are left out: root `tasks/backlog/` (resume exam, attempt history,
weak-spot drill, mistake deck, flag for review, exam-day countdown, best per CTFL set, export/import,
sync) and the open ideas in `general-whole-app`, `study-experience` and `new-learning-track`.
